Start with the true statement 2 < 6. Multiply both sides by −1. Is −2 < −6 still true?

No — −2 is greater than −6. Multiplying or dividing by a negative flips the inequality sign.

An inequality (>, <, ≥, ≤) describes a range of values, not a single one. Solving is almost identical to equations — with one crucial twist.

An inequality (>, <, ≥, ≤) describes a whole range of solutions rather than a single value — a stretch of the number line instead of one point on it.

Reading the ends: open or filled

  • A strict inequality (<, >) excludes its endpoint: for x < 5, the value 5 is not a solution.
  • ≤ and ≥ include theirs: for x ≥ 3, the value 3 is a solution — and questions are often decided exactly there.
  • Many real limits are ranges, not single numbers — a lift limit w ≤ 500 kg, a ride needing h ≥ 120 cm.

The one exception: multiplying or dividing by a negative

  1. Solve exactly as you would an equation — the same legal moves apply.
  2. The moment you multiply or divide by a negative, flip the sign. For example, −2x < 6 → divide by −2 → x > −3.
  3. Draw the solution on a number line, choosing an open or filled circle to match the sign.
  4. Test one value from inside your range in the original inequality. If it fails, the sign was flipped the wrong way.
The exception× or ÷ by a NEGATIVE → reverse the inequality sign
🚗 A speed limit sign

A sign reads "speed ≤ 50 km/h". Any speed from 0 up to and including 50 is legal — not only exactly 50.

🎡 A rollercoaster height rule

A ride requires height ≥ 120 cm. A rider exactly 120 cm tall is allowed on; someone even 1 cm shorter is turned away.

🍿 A party snack budget

You have $150 for snacks at $6 each: 6n ≤ 150 → n ≤ 25. You can buy up to 25 items without going over budget.

The one special rule: multiplying or dividing by a negative flips the sign. On a number line, negating both sides reverses their order (2 < 5 but −2 > −5).
⚠ Watch outOnly flip the sign when you multiply or divide by a negative — not when you simply add or subtract a negative number.
⚡ Mental maths strategySolve exactly like an equation, but say aloud “flip the sign if I multiply or divide by a negative” at every step.
